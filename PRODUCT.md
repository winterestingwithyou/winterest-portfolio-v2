# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary users:** People evaluating Winterest's work and experience, including hiring teams, potential clients, and collaborators. They need to understand the work and decide whether to make contact.
- **Secondary users:** Winterest and trusted collaborators who maintain portfolio content through the private dashboard. Their permissions depend on their assigned role.

## Product Purpose

Winterest's primary portfolio presents projects, experience, education, and technical interests, and gives visitors a way to make contact. Its integrated CMS lets Winterest and trusted collaborators maintain that information. Success means visitors can quickly understand Winterest's capabilities, relevant inquiries and opportunities follow, and portfolio content stays current.

## Positioning

This is Winterest's official primary portfolio, replacing previous portfolio sites. It combines a public portfolio with a private content-management dashboard and represents Winterest's practical work building web systems and developer tools.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the public portfolio to evaluate Winterest's work, explore project details and technical interests, review available career and education information, and make contact. Winterest and trusted collaborators use the private dashboard to maintain portfolio content and related site information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public site presents portfolio projects, case studies, profile and career information, education, technical interests, contact options, and a resume when one is available.
- The private dashboard supports content management for authorized users with owner, admin, or editor permissions.
- The site supports English and Indonesian.
- Public content and claims must be based on real information. Do not invent projects, experience, testimonials, statistics, or other proof.
- The site must remain responsive and accessible across its supported devices and interaction methods.
- The existing web application uses Bun and TanStack Start and is deployed to Cloudflare Workers. Future work must preserve compatibility with that platform.

## Brand Commitments

- The public brand is **Winterest**.
- This is the primary personal portfolio and CMS platform for Winterest.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains portfolio project information, profile and career details, education information, technical interests, and site contact and resume configuration. Use existing or user-provided content as evidence. Treat testimonials and numerical claims as unverified unless the user supplies supporting sources.

## Product Principles

- Help visitors understand Winterest's capabilities through real work and experience.
- Make it straightforward for interested visitors to get in touch.
- Let Winterest and trusted collaborators keep portfolio information current through the CMS.
- Keep content truthful and available to English- and Indonesian-speaking visitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site and dashboard must be responsive and accessible, including keyboard-operable interactions, visible focus, readable contrast, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.
