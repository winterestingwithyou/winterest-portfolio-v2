## Direction contract

THESIS: Make earned credentials easy to scan and verify, with the certificate document—not decorative UI—as the proof.

OWN-WORLD: Extend the established Winterest Cloudflare + Bun Flat Precision system: solid neutral surfaces, hairline borders, restrained orange for interaction, and consistent light/dark themes.

STORY: Visitors scan the newest credentials, understand who issued them and when, then open the certificate or verify it with its issuer. The homepage offers a direct, manual carousel preview of up to five recent entries.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The Certificates page opens with a clear localized heading and newest-first certificate cards. On home, the section heading stays in the site container while the carousel rail spans the viewport; the active card is larger and neighboring cards peek in at reduced scale.

FORM: Use a scan-friendly certificate list and a looping, no-autoplay Embla carousel. Use no arrow buttons or 3D rotation; support touch, pointer drag, focused keyboard navigation, passive position indicators, and reduced-motion preferences.

FINISH: Complete the page and homepage section, verify mobile and desktop layouts and carousel interactions, and document the shipped requirements.
