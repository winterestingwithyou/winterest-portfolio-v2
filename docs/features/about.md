# Feature: About & Personal Journey

## Description

The About page introduces Winterest through personal background, engineering values, career and education history, and selected interests.

## Requirements

- The page is public and presents Winterest's background, engineering approach, and personal profile.
- A chronological journey presents personal and professional milestones.
- Work and education sections show the available career and academic history.
- Personal interests include K-pop favorites and gaming influences, with music links leading to their intended destinations.
- Content is available in English and Indonesian.
- The page and its timeline remain accessible and usable on mobile and desktop.
